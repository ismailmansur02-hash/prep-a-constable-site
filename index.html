How you use it

Three things it's good for.

Mocks

Sit the mock before you sit the real one.

The clock runs and nothing is marked until you submit. When your real result comes back, you can record it next to your practice scores and see how close the mocks were.

  • AP120 questions, 30 minutes
  • AP2, AP3, AP440 questions, 60 minutes
  • Custom mockyour own topics and length

Verbal Drills

Say the caution to your phone, not the mirror.

Three drills: the caution, GOWISELY for a stop and search, and the ESD arrest wording after a failed roadside breath test. The screen goes blank while you speak, so you can't read it off. Afterwards it shows what you missed.

Somewhere you can't talk? Type it instead.

Topics

One Act at a time, until it sticks.

Open a topic, read the short lesson, then answer its questions. Questions are scheduled for review, and there's a daily question goal if you want one.

Your progress is stored on the phone.

Constable Companion

The law itself, in your pocket.

151 offences and 25 powers, each with its section, its points to prove and its penalty. Search by the name you know it by, and the right card comes up.

Behind them sit 740 cards with the wording of the Acts, quoted from legislation.gov.uk, so you revise from the real text and not from somebody's summary.

Questions

Straight answers

Is this an official police app?

No. Prep a Constable is an independent revision app. It is not produced, endorsed or approved by any police force, the Metropolitan Police Service, the Home Office or the College of Policing.

Who is it for?

Anyone preparing for the constable assessments in England and Wales: AP1, AP2, AP3 and AP4.

What does it cost?

£6.99, once. No subscription and nothing to buy inside the app.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use the whole app without one. Sign in with Apple, Google or an email link if you want your progress on more than one device.

Is the law up to date?

The wording on the legislation cards was taken from legislation.gov.uk between 25 October and 9 November 2026, and the app shows that date. It is a study aid, so check the live legislation before you rely on a point.

Can I practise the caution out loud?

Yes. Say it to your phone and the app shows the words you missed. You can also build up to it in parts, or type it when you can't talk.
