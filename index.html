Features

What is inside

The numbers below are counted from the same material the app ships.

Questions and lessons

885 exam-style questions across 35 topics, and 136 short lessons. Every question includes an explanation.

Mock exams

AP1: 20 questions in 30 minutes. AP2, AP3, AP4: 40 questions in 60 minutes. Nothing is marked until you submit. You can build a custom mock, and record a real assessment result next to your practice scores.

Flashcards

333 cards. Tap a card to turn it over. Got it takes it out of the deck. Again sends it to the back. 40 mnemonics sit in Reference, separate from the Companion.

Verbal Drills

Three spoken drills: the caution, GOWISELY for a stop and search, and the ESD arrest wording after a failed roadside breath test. The screen goes blank while you speak. You can type it instead. Afterwards you see what was missed.

Constable Companion

151 offences and 25 powers, with points to prove. 155 legislation cards quote the text from legislation.gov.uk. Law as at 25 October 2026.

Progress

A daily question goal and a study streak. After you have answered some questions, the home screen shows a review queue and the topics you are weakest on. Progress is stored on the phone.

How it works

Four ways to revise

  1. Study a topic

    Open one of the 35 topics, read the short lesson, then practise its questions.

  2. Keep what you miss

    Flashcards you mark Again come back. Questions are scheduled for review, and weak topics can be drilled from the home screen.

  3. Sit a timed mock

    AP1: 20 questions in 30 minutes. AP2, AP3, AP4: 40 questions in 60 minutes. It runs under exam conditions, with no feedback until you submit.

  4. Say it, then look it up

    Deliver the caution, GOWISELY or the ESD arrest wording out loud, or type it. Use the Companion when you need the offence, the power and the quoted legislation.

The law in the app

Quoted, labelled, and dated.

Law as at 25 October 2026

Where a Companion card has legislation behind it, the wording is quoted from legislation.gov.uk and reused under the Open Government Licence. The date above is the date that text was checked to.

155 legislation cards are listed for the law as it applies. 39 more stay hidden until you choose to include changes in the law: text that only amends another Act, provisions that are not yet in force, and repealed provisions.

Points to prove, penalty lines and notes are compiled from that quoted text and labelled as compiled. The quoted text is the authority. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

This is a study aid. It is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for the legislation, a force's own policy, or College of Policing guidance. Check the live source before you rely on a point.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is this an official police app?

Not an official police app. Prep a Constable is not produced, endorsed or approved by any police force, the Metropolitan Police Service, the Home Office or the College of Policing. It is a revision aid published by Arim Capital Ltd.

Who is it for?

People preparing for the Police Constable Entry Programme and the Detective Constable Entry Programme assessments in England and Wales: AP1, AP2, AP3 and AP4.

What does it cost?

£6.99 one-off, including any applicable VAT. No subscription and nothing renews. There are no purchases inside the app.

Do I need an account?

No. Sign-in, if you want it, is with Apple, Google, or a one-time email link. There is no password. Guest progress stays on that phone.

How is the law kept current?

Legislation on the Companion cards is quoted from legislation.gov.uk and marked with the date it was checked to, currently 25 October 2026. Compiled notes are labelled as compiled. The app is a study aid, not an official source. Check the live legislation before you rely on it.

What happens to microphone audio?

The microphone is used only in Verbal Drills, and only while you are recording. Every drill can be typed instead. Where the phone can transcribe on the device, the audio stays there. Where it cannot, the audio is sent to Apple or Google — whichever provides the phone's speech service — to be turned into text. The app says which of the two applies before the microphone opens. We never receive or store the audio. The text is used to mark that drill and is not saved.

Can my answers be used to improve the app?

Only if you leave "Help improve the app" on. That produces combined figures across many people, such as which topics are hardest, and never a report about you. A figure covering fewer than 20 people is not produced. Turn the control off in Profile and your study data is left out.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile, tap Delete account, then Delete forever. You can also use the account deletion page or email us. Deletion in the app removes the account immediately. An email request is completed within 30 days.

Does this website use cookies?

No. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising or other trackers. The privacy notice linked from the footer is the notice for the app.
