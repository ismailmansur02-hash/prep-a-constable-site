Common questions

Refunds. Purchases are made through Apple or Google, who take the payment, so refunds are requested from them. Your statutory rights are set out in the Terms.

Deleting your account and data. In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account. The account deletion page sets out the steps, what is deleted and what is kept. You can also email us.

Complaints. Email us with the subject "Complaint". We acknowledge within 30 days and tell you the outcome. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Spotted a mistake in the law? Please tell us the section and what you think it should say. Do not send operational information, case details, or personal information about a member of the public.
