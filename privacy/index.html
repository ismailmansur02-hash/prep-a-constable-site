Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Prep a Constable is a revision app for people preparing for United Kingdom police entry examinations. This notice explains what personal information we collect, why, what we do with it, and the rights you have over it. We collect the minimum needed to run the app and this website.

Who is responsible for your information

The data controller is Arim Capital Ltd, company number 17574560, registered in England and Wales. Our registered office is 128 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX, United Kingdom and our website is https://prepaconstable.co.uk. You can contact us about anything in this notice, including any request about your personal information, at our registered office or by email:

support@prepaconstable.co.uk

What we collect, why, and our lawful basis

Your email address. Collected only if you choose to create an account. You can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with a one-time sign-in link. We use the address to identify your account so your progress can sync between your devices. Lawful basis: performance of a contract — it is necessary to provide the account service you asked for. There is no password, so we never hold one.

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your email address with us. Where you use Hide My Email, Apple shares a private relay address instead of your personal inbox (the address ends in privaterelay.appleid.com) and forwards our messages to you. If you choose Google, Google shares your email address with us. If you choose the email link, we collect the address you type and use it to send that one-time sign-in link. We never receive an Apple or Google password.

Your first name, surname and rank, if you choose to enter them. Used only to address you in the app. These are optional and you can change or clear them at any time in Profile. Lawful basis: performance of a contract.

Your study data: which questions you have answered and whether you got them right, questions you have flagged, mock attempt history, lessons marked as read, your spaced-repetition schedule, your daily streak, your chosen Assessment Point and dates, and any real assessment results you record. This is what the app is for — it produces your progress, your weak spots and your revision schedule. Lawful basis: performance of a contract.

Speech, only while a Verbal Drill is recording. See the separate section below, because what happens to it depends on your device. Lawful basis: your consent, which you give by tapping the microphone button and granting the permission your device asks for. You can withdraw it at any time by not using the microphone, by using the "Type it instead" option, or by turning the permission off in your device settings.

Whether the app is working. We look at how people do overall — average accuracy, whether mock scores improve with practice, and which topics people find hardest — so we can tell whether the app actually helps and fix the parts that do not. These checks use only study data you have already given us, turned into combined figures. They are separate from the usage statistics below, and they collect nothing extra for this purpose. We only ever produce combined figures across many people, never a report about you, and any figure covering fewer than 20 people is not produced at all. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in knowing whether the app works and in improving it. You can object at any time by turning off "Help improve the app" in Profile, and your data is then excluded entirely.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photographs, your camera, your device advertising identifier, your browsing behaviour outside the app, or any operational policing information. The app contains no advertising and does not follow you across other companies' apps or websites. Anonymous usage statistics are described below and are on unless you turn them off. The website is different: see the Website section below. We do not build a profile of you and no decision about you is made automatically.

On the iPhone and Android apps, if the app hits an error, it sends an error report so we can fix it. About 5% of performance measurements are sent as well, so we can see when the app is slow. These go to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), which acts as our processor. A report contains the app version, the build number, the kind of device and its operating system, and a technical description of the fault. It does not include your name, your email address or your IP address, and it is not linked to your account. If you tap Report a problem in Profile, only the message you type is sent. That form does not ask for your name or email and does not attach a screenshot. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in keeping the app working. The website does not send these reports.

We do not ask for or want special category information — anything revealing health, ethnicity, religion, political opinions, trade union membership, sex life, sexual orientation, genetic or biometric data. Please do not enter any real case information, intelligence, or personal information about any member of the public into this app.

Usage statistics

On the iPhone and Android apps, anonymous usage statistics are on unless you turn them off. Their only purpose is to understand, in combined figures, which parts of the app are used and where people get stuck, so we can improve the app and fix problems. We do not use them to advertise, to sell information, or to follow you across other companies' apps or websites.

The software is PostHog, provided by PostHog Inc. PostHog acts only as our processor: it may use the information only to provide that service to us and on our instructions, and not for its own purposes. The statistics are stored in the European Union, in Frankfurt. What is sent is an anonymous identifier stored on the device, the events listed below, the app version, the device model and the operating system. We do not send your name, your email address or your account id. We do not send the text of any question or anything you type. We do not record your screen and we do not record your taps. The app does not add your IP address or your location to the event, and it tells PostHog not to work out a location from the connection. No profile of you is created.

The events are: the app was opened; the first time statistics run on a device; an account was created or someone signed in, recorded only as Apple, Google or email; a practice or a mock was started; and a practice or a mock was finished, with the topic where there is one topic, the number of questions, the score as a percentage, and whether it was practice or a mock.

Storing that identifier on your device, without asking first, is covered by the statistical-purposes exception in paragraph 5 of Schedule A1 to the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations. That paragraph was added by section 112 of the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 and has been in force since 5 February 2026. The only purpose is statistical information about how the service is used. You are given this notice, and you can object simply and free of charge. Lawful basis for using the statistics under the UK GDPR: our legitimate interests in improving the app, limited to these combined statistics. The switch is on unless you turn it off. Turn off "Share anonymous usage stats" in Profile. Turning it off stops any further statistics from this app immediately.

The microphone and your speech

The microphone is used in one place only: Verbal Drills, and only while you are recording. It is never used at any other time and never in the background. Every drill can be completed by typing instead.

Where your device can transcribe speech offline, the audio is turned into text on the device and never leaves it. Where your device cannot, the audio is sent to Apple or Google — whichever provides your device's speech service — to be turned into text, and is handled under their own privacy terms. The app tells you which of the two applies to you on the screen before the microphone opens.

Either way we never receive, record, store or upload the audio. The text is held only in the app's memory while the drill is on screen, is used only to mark the drill, is not saved to your device and is not sent to us. Your drill score is not stored either.

Who else receives your information

Supabase, our database and authentication provider, stores your account and study data on our behalf. They act as our processor, which means they may only use it to provide that service to us and on our instructions.

Apple, if you sign in with Apple. Apple shares your email address, or a private relay address where Hide My Email applies, as described above. Google, if you sign in with Google. Google shares your email address. Neither shares your password with us. They handle that sign-in under their own privacy terms.

Apple or Google, if your device sends speech for transcription as described above, and when you buy the app. They act under their own privacy terms for those purposes.

Cloudflare, if you visit this website. Cloudflare provides Web Analytics for the site and acts as our processor, which means they may only use the information to provide that service to us and on our instructions. They do not receive information from the app for this purpose.

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), on the iPhone and Android apps only, receives the error reports, performance measurements and problem reports described above. Sentry acts as our processor and may only use them to provide that service to us and on our instructions. These reports are stored in Germany.

PostHog (PostHog Inc.) processes the anonymous usage statistics from the iPhone and Android apps, and the cookieless page statistics from this website, described above and in the Website section. PostHog acts only as our processor and may use them only to provide that service to us and on our instructions. They are stored in Frankfurt, in the European Union. No profile of a person is created.

We may disclose information if we are legally required to. Beyond that, nobody else receives your personal information. We do not sell it, share it for anyone else's marketing, or transfer it to advertisers. Ever.

Where your information is held

Our database is hosted in Ireland, inside the European Economic Area. Error reports from the iPhone and Android apps are stored in Germany, also inside the European Economic Area. Anonymous usage statistics from the apps, and the cookieless statistics from this website, are stored in Frankfurt, also inside the European Economic Area. Transfers from the United Kingdom to the European Economic Area are covered by the United Kingdom's adequacy regulations, so no additional transfer safeguard is required for them.

Some of our providers are based outside the United Kingdom and may access information from there in order to support the service. Where that happens we rely on the transfer safeguards in our agreements with them, such as the International Data Transfer Agreement or the United Kingdom Addendum to the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for details.

If you visit the website, Cloudflare processes the information described in the Website section below.

How long we keep it

Your account and study data are kept until you delete your account. When you delete it, the account and all associated data are removed immediately from our database and are not recoverable.

We delete your data when you delete your account, or on request to support@prepaconstable.co.uk.

Speech is not retained at all. Data held only on your device is removed when you delete the app, except the sign-in session, which the iPhone keeps in its Keychain and Apple may leave in place after an uninstall; signing out or deleting your account removes it. Reset progress clears your study data immediately.

Error reports and problem reports are not kept with your account, because they are not linked to you. Deleting your account does not delete them. They are kept only so we can fix the app. If you sent a problem report and want it deleted, contact us and we will ask Sentry to delete it.

Anonymous usage statistics are not kept with your account, because they are not linked to you. PostHog keeps events for its default period: one year on the free plan, and two years on a pay-as-you-go plan that started on or after 5 November 2026. No person profile is created, so there is no profile to keep. Turning the statistics off stops new events from the app. Events already sent are removed when that period ends. Deleting your account does not delete them. The website statistics kept by PostHog are kept for the same period.

The combined website analytics in our Cloudflare dashboard are kept for about six months.

Your rights

Under the United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to be informed about how your information is used, which is what this notice is for; to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you; to have inaccurate information corrected; to have your information erased; to restrict how we use it; to object to our using it; and to receive it in a portable, machine-readable form. Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing it does not affect anything done before you did.

You can exercise most of these immediately in the app. Profile lets you see and change everything we hold about you, and Profile then Delete account erases it. For anything else, contact us by email:

support@prepaconstable.co.uk

We will respond within one month. We may extend that by up to two further months for complex requests, and we will tell you within the first month if we need to. We do not charge for this.

If you are unhappy

You have the right to complain to us about how we have used your personal information. Contact us by email, with the word Complaint in the subject line, and tell us what has gone wrong:

support@prepaconstable.co.uk

We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days of receiving it, look into it, keep you informed of progress, and tell you the outcome.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the United Kingdom's data protection regulator, at any time. Their helpline is 0303 123 1113 and their website is ico.org.uk. You do not have to come to us first, and complaining to either of us does not affect any other legal remedy.

Marketing

We do not send marketing. The only email we send is the one-time sign-in link, when you ask for one. We send no push notifications. If you switch on the exam-day reminder, your phone shows one notification at 6pm on the assessment date you entered, asking how it went; it is scheduled on your phone, nothing is sent to us for it, and you can switch it off in Exam Prep or in your phone's settings.

Cookies and tracking

The app uses no cookies and no advertising identifiers. It does not follow you across other companies' apps or websites. Anonymous usage statistics are described above. They are on unless you turn off Share anonymous usage stats in Profile. The app stores your progress on your own device so it works offline, and stores your sign-in session so you are not asked to sign in every time. Both are necessary to provide the service you have asked for.

This website sets no cookies and does not use advertising identifiers or fingerprinting. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics and PostHog for combined statistics, as described in the Website section below. PostHog on the website keeps its identifier in memory only, for that visit, and does not use cookies or local storage.

Website

This section is about the marketing website at https://prepaconstable.co.uk, not the app. Anonymous usage statistics in the app are described above and are on unless you turn them off in Profile. The app contains no advertising. Error reports from the iPhone and Android apps are described above.

If you visit the website, we use two services to see, in combined figures, how the site is used.

Cloudflare Web Analytics shows, for example, how many times each page is viewed, which sites people came from, and the countries and types of device represented. It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint you, and does not build a profile of you. We are not given a report that identifies you. Cloudflare provides this service and acts as our processor, which means they may only use the information to provide that service to us and on our instructions. To do this Cloudflare briefly processes technical details your browser sends, such as your IP address, the page address and your browser type, and may do so outside the United Kingdom, under the transfer safeguards described above. The figures in our Cloudflare dashboard are kept for about six months.

PostHog, provided by PostHog Inc., also counts page views on the website, so we can see which pages are used and improve the site. It is stored in Frankfurt, in the European Union. PostHog acts only as our processor. On the website it does not use cookies and does not use local storage. The identifier lasts only for that visit, in the browser's memory, and no profile of you is created. It does not record the session and it does not record what you type. It does not run if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. That is how you object to the website statistics. There is no account on the website to switch off.

Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in understanding how the website is used and in improving it, and, for the storage on your device, the statistical-purposes exception in paragraph 5 of Schedule A1 to the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations. Because the figures are combined, we cannot use them to identify you or to act on any request about you individually. A Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal stops the PostHog statistics on that browser.

Children

This app is intended for adults preparing for police recruitment and is not directed at children. Applicants to United Kingdom police forces must be at least 18 at appointment. We do not knowingly collect information from children, we ask for no more information from any user than the app needs, and none of the features that pose particular risks to children — location, profiling, personalised advertising, public profiles and contact between users — exist in this app at all. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Our database enforces row-level security so that each account can only ever read and write its own data. There is no password on the account: email sign-in is a one-time link, and Apple and Google sign-in are handled by those providers. The key that ships inside the app is a public, restricted key and grants no access to anyone else's information.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your information we will update this notice and the date at the top, and where the change is significant we will tell you in the app.
